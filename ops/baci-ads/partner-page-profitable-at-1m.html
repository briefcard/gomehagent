Profitable at $1M a year

The first goal is a profitable $1M year. It's reachable, and it depends mainly on one number: online ad spend as a share of online sales. Today it's 59%. Below about 44% the business makes money at $1M; at 30% it makes about $123K a year with B2B at 40% of sales. Run as it is today, a $1M year loses about $193K.

What changed in this version
  • B2B is the Baci Backoffice channel ($14,235, at keystone), draft orders ($9,746, at about 19% off retail) and Faire ($2,054): $26,035, or 20% of sales. Direct sales are the online store, Instagram, Shop and phone orders: $101,988. The earlier "hold wholesale at keystone" lever is withdrawn: wholesale is already at or above keystone.
  • Chargebacks and returns are included at your rate: 3% of online orders, each losing the sale plus $15, the goods and the shipping.
  • Free product is counted: draft orders at 100% off (giveaways, event product, gifts, reshipments) totalled at least $11,645 at retail in the last 12 months, about $3,600 at cost.
  • Kept from earlier versions: duty and freight measured from the customs entries and invoices; $8 labels, paid by the customer below $110; the $4,000 employee and the $2,500 warehouse including its staff; the ≈$50K Shoppe Object show.

Each change: the data, the change, the work

Values are at $1M, in the order applied above.

Cap online ads at 30% of online sales

+$232K
Data pulled
Meta spent $60,243 in 12 months, 59.1% of direct sales. Meta claims $68,299 of sales; Shopify counts $41,563 arriving from social media, or $0.69 per $1 spent. Month to month Meta's own count ran from $0.08 back per $1 (March) to $1.83 (December).
Change
Each month's budget is 30% of the prior month's online sales, giveaways included. Cut cold-audience campaigns in any month returning under about $2 per $1 on Shopify's count. Put the effort into sales that don't need ads: a second-purchase email series, the recovery emails, email to the list, private clients.
Work
A weekly spend check (low). Building email and repeat buying is ongoing marketing work.
Confidence
Medium The saving is arithmetic. Growing online sales 6× at this ratio is the unproven part.

If the ad gate fails by 31 January

If spending can't come down without online sales falling with it, the remaining paths are to run Baci as a B2B distributor for the US and Canada with little or no paid advertising (B2B keeps 37–42¢ per $1), or to wind it down.
