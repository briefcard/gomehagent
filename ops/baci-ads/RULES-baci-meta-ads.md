# Baci Milano USA — Meta ads standing rules

Owner-set rules for ad account 949335500698690. Any session or person
touching this account checks a change against these rules **before** it
goes live. Companion to `HANDOFF-baci-meta-ads-2026-11-02.md`
(branch `claude/sleepy-mendel-lpoz4j`).

## The rules

1. **$40 is the maximum cost per sale. Non-negotiable.**
   Never raise the Scale cost cap above $40. If Scale under-spends at $40,
   the fix is more ads that convert under $40, not a higher cap.

2. **Kill rule for test ads (no cost cap on tests).**
   Pause any prospecting/test ad that spends **$80 (2 × the $40 limit)
   without a sale**. A test ad that keeps selling above $40 per sale after
   enough spend to judge is also paused, never graduated.

3. **A winner runs in one place only.**
   Once an ad graduates to Scale, it is removed from testing and
   prospecting. The same ad or post must never run in two campaigns aimed
   at the same people. They bid against each other, and the uncapped copy
   starves the capped one.

4. **Testing is the small share of spend.**
   Prospecting/testing at about $100–110/day while Scale is $150/day. Ad set
   minimums inside the test campaign must total well under its budget
   (currently $30 × 3 = $90 of $110).

5. **Changes happen on Mondays only**, and only after checking them
   against these rules. Every live change is read back from Meta to
   confirm it actually took effect.

6. **Never publish the Ads Manager draft wholesale.** The account carries
   an unpublished draft (the Nov 2 Scale restructure, Gift Guide ad,
   "Trial Reels 2", about 66 staged edits). Publish only the exact objects
   the owner approved.

## Why (Nov 2026)

From Nov 3, prospecting ran at $260/day with $210 of forced minimums and
no cap. It included a copy of Scale's top ad ("Most Personal"), shown to
the same women aged 30–64. Scale's best ad went from $44.50 to $122 per
sale, its $40 cap throttled spend from about $175/day to under $15/day,
and account sales fell from about 6/day to about 2/day.
